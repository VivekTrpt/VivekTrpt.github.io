About Me.

I am an Electrical Engineering researcher working in control systems, cyber-physical systems, power-system control, microgrids, and resilient control. My research focuses on advanced control methodologies, including PID-based control, fractional-order control, internal model control (IMC), Smith predictor-based control, and active disturbance rejection control (ADRC), with particular emphasis on the detection and mitigation of cyber-attacks in modern power and energy systems.
