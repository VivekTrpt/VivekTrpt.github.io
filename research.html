Research

My research focuses on secure and resilient control of power systems and cyber-physical systems under disturbances, uncertainties, communication delays, and cyber attacks.

Cyber-Attack Detection & Mitigation

Detection and mitigation strategies for time-delay, scaling, false-data and coordinated cyber threats.

Power-System Control

Load-frequency control, interconnected systems, renewable integration and microgrids.

Advanced Control

PID variants, internal model control, ADRC, fractional-order control and robustness analysis.

Real-Time Validation

Real-time and hardware validation of proposed control strategies using the OPAL-RT OP5600 real-time simulator and the FPGA-based WAVECT WCU300 Controller.
