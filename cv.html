Vivek Kumar

Ph.D. Researcher (Senior Research Fellow), Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. My research is centered on control systems, cyber-physical systems, power-system applications, microgrids, and resilient control, with particular emphasis on the analysis and mitigation of cyber-attacks.

Research Interests

Advanced and conventional control-system design, including PID and its variants, fractional-order PID control, Internal Model Control (IMC), frequency-shifted IMC, Smith predictor-based control, and Active Disturbance Rejection Control (ADRC). My work also includes process control, power-system applications, dynamic-system modelling, and resilience analysis of cyber-physical systems under cyber-attacks such as time-delay, false-data-injection, scaling, and Man-in-the-Middle attacks.
